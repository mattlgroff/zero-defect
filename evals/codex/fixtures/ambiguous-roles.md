# Draft responsibilities for sponsor approval

The required scope is approval of changes, delivery completion, and transition to support.

Account Lead owns final approval of every change. Finance, Engineering, Operations, and Customer Success each advise on change requests. Delivery Lead also owns final approval of every change. No joint approval, threshold split, or escalation rule is defined.

Delivery Lead owns delivery completion. Customer Success manages transition readiness and Operations takes it from there. These descriptions do not specify whether Customer Success or Operations is accountable for transition completion, when the transfer occurs, or what the receiving role must accept.
