# Approved decision authority

This reference records approved internal authority. Its scope is commercial change approval for nonnegative change values and the release go/no-go decision. Delivery execution and ongoing operations are outside this document's scope. No external commitments are made here.

For commercial changes below $10,000, the Account Lead is the accountable approval owner. For changes of $10,000 or more, the Sponsor is the accountable approval owner. The Delivery Lead assesses feasibility and advises the relevant approval owner; the Delivery Lead does not hold final commercial approval authority. Changes cannot proceed until the applicable owner records approval in the change log.

The Steering Committee is the accountable owner of the release go/no-go decision. Finance and Delivery Lead are its two members. Both members must attend. Each has one vote. A unanimous vote records the committee's decision. A tie or a missing member is escalated to the Sponsor for a final decision before release. Committee membership does not create separate individual go/no-go approval authority. The committee chair records the decision in the change log and informs the release team.
